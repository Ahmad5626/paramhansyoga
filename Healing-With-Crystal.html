COURSE DETAILS

All ancient cultures of the world have used crystals in one way or the other for their mystical properties. Native Americans call crystals and other rocks “stone people” and regard them respectfully as our “oldest ancestors”. To the Mayans the crystal was sacred to their Gods and was used for both divining and healing. Hindus, Christians, Persians, Babylonians and Egyptians all used crystals to various purposes. What gives crystals their mystical properties ? Only one thing that Crystals have Consciousness that can be programmed.
