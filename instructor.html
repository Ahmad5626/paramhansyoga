ABOUT MR. ARUN KUMAR

  • Mr. Arun Kumar is a ‘Senior Arhat Trainer’ who has been practising this healing modality since 2002—during his college years.
  • Graduated from MVIT Bangalore with a Master’s in Computer Applications.
  • Worked for Multi-National Corporations before joining WISSEN INFOTECH (Bangalore).
  • Quit his corporate job in 2009 to dedicate himself to healing and teaching.
  • Has been spreading teachings across India (Bihar, UP, Delhi, Nepal, Jharkhand, Orissa, Jammu & Kashmir) and abroad (UK, Canada).
  • Produced over 100 trainers in the field of Arhat Healing.
  • Conducted international programs in Bodhgaya and Rajgir (Bihar) with over 500 participants.
  • Leads higher spiritual courses in Vaishali, the birthplace of Lord Mahaveer.
  • Engages in charitable works, providing free education and spiritual teachings to underprivileged children.
  • Aims to eliminate suffering and lead humanity towards spiritual enlightenment.
