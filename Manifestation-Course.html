SYNOPSIS

Ever wondered why some of your reasonable dreams do not come true? Why do you sometimes do not get appropriate returns for your investments? Why do you not get the fruit of your labour? This fills our life with survival-anxiety while we, being the children of the most High, should inherit a life of prosperity. This course familiarises you with all of the laws of manifestation so that your ability to manifest is with more precision. Learn techniques of visualisation and energy magnification to bring your desires to materialisation more rapidly.
